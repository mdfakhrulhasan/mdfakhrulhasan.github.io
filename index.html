About me

I majored in Statistics at the University of Rajshahi and am now an Assistant Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Applied Statistics at Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU), where I have taught since December 2022. I teach applied statistics with R, SPSS and Stata. My research uses regression, panel-data and time-series methods on health, climate and agricultural data from Bangladesh.

My M.Sc. thesis analysed maternal and paternal risk factors for autism spectrum disorder, using community survey data from northern Bangladesh. At BAU, I was Principal Investigator of a project funded by Bangladesh’s Ministry of Science and Technology, studying how climate-related stresses constrain rice production in southern Bangladesh. My recent work relates climate and weather variation to crop production, including a district-level panel study of coastal Bangladesh.

Current directionI now want to apply these methods to urban health: how flooding, heat and water stress affect people living in the river and coastal cities of Bangladesh, and how routine health and climate records can become evidence that city planners can act on.
