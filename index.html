About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Agricultural and Applied Statistics at Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU), where I have taught since December 2022. I teach applied statistics with R, SPSS and Stata. My research uses regression, panel-data and time-series methods on health, climate and agricultural data from Bangladesh.

My M.Sc. thesis at the University of Rajshahi analysed maternal and parental risk factors for autism spectrum disorder, using community survey data from northern Bangladesh. At BAU, I led a project funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology as Principal Investigator, studying how climate-related stresses constrain rice production in southern Bangladesh. My recent papers relate climate and weather variation to crop production, including a district-level panel study of coastal Bangladesh.

Current directionI now want to apply these methods to urban health: how flooding, heat and water stress affect people living in the river and coastal cities of Bangladesh, and how routine health and climate records can become evidence that city planners can act on.
