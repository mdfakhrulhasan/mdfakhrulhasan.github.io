About me

I have taught in the Department of Agricultural and Applied Statistics at Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU) since December 2022, first as Lecturer and now as Assistant Professor. I hold B.Sc. (Honours) and M.Sc. degrees in Statistics from the University of Rajshahi. I teach applied statistics with R, SPSS and Stata. My research uses regression, panel-data and time-series methods on health, climate and agricultural data from Bangladesh.

My M.Sc. thesis analysed maternal and parental factors associated with autism severity among children with autism spectrum disorder, using community survey data from northern Bangladesh. At BAU, I was Principal Investigator of a project funded by Bangladesh’s Ministry of Science and Technology, studying how climate-related stresses constrain rice production in southern Bangladesh.
